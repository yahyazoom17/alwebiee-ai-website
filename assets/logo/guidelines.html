Clear space

Keep one woven-band width clear on every side. That is ≈ 26 units here — 15% of the symbol's height.

The band is 28 units wide against 186 units of symbol, so the zone always scales with the logo. Never use a fixed pixel margin.

Nothing enters the zone — no text, no rules, no other marks, no image edges.

Approved pairs & misuse

Do

  • White artwork on Alwebiee Green (5.36:1) — the primary pair
  • Black on white for documents
  • Green on white, white on Ink
  • On photos: over a calm area or in a solid green square

Don't

  • Don't stretch, squash or rotate
  • Don't recolour outside the palette
  • Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or glows
  • Don't rearrange or resize parts of a lockup
  • Don't retype the wordmark or crop the frame
